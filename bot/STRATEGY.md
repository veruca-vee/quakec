# Vee's genuine rules — the strategy layer (her words, 2026-11-26)

These are Vee's own CoD Zombies rules, given verbally for the bot.
They outrank generic bot heuristics.

## Round 1 — points farming at the windows
- Wait for zombies to get TO the window. Do not waste them at range.
- Per zombie: **4 shots in a non-lethal spot, then knife them.**
  (She corrected herself mid-sentence — "just knife them" was wrong;
  final ruling: "what I said first was correct, four shots, knife them.")
- That shot+knife combo is deliberate: maximum points per zombie.
- Reload, then run window to window checking which windows have
  zombies coming.

## Between rounds 1 and 2
- Repair the windows (boards = points + breathing room).

## Round 2
- Per zombie: **8 shots, then knife.** Two knives if it's an emergency.

## After round 2
- Open a door. Start letting them leak through the windows instead of
  holding every window forever.
- Buy a rifle. Aim for heads from here on.

## Mystery box (later)
- Explosive weapons are a self-kill hazard for a bot: never fire an
  explosive at close range / own position. Treat box explosives as
  guilty until proven safe.

## The endgame she actually wants
- Once the bot lives in the code, she is effectively a **co-op
  partner**: "you play two player without somebody else."
- The in-code route is also the lag fix: no cloud-browser control
  loop, no frame parsing — the script runs inside the game on
  whatever device opens it.
